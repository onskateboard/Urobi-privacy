Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Urobi app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Robert Schirmer (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location). No account registration is required to use the Application.

To provide its services, the Application communicates with a backend server operated by the Service Provider. The backend stores the following data linked to an anonymous device identifier:

  • Anonymous device ID — a randomly generated identifier (UUID) assigned to your device, not linked to your name, Apple ID, or any personal identity.
  • Tier & credit balance — whether your device is on the free or standard tier, and your current credit balance.
  • Usage logs — per-request records including the AI model used, token counts, estimated cost, and timestamp. Retained for billing and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Credit transaction history — an audit ledger of free grants, purchases, and usage debits.
  • Security tokens — a refresh token (valid for 30 days) and a per-device HMAC secret used to authenticate requests. These contain no personal information.
  • App version & last seen — used for compatibility and support purposes.

No images, voice recordings, or AI-generated text are stored on the Service Provider's servers.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

When you actively use AI-assisted features, camera images (resized), voice transcriptions (text only, transcribed on-device), and generated text for speech synthesis are routed through the Service Provider's backend and forwarded to OpenAI, L.L.C., a third-party AI service provider located in the United States. This occurs only when you actively use these features and only to provide the requested functionality.

OpenAI is required to handle your data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy, which provides protections equivalent to or greater than those described in this policy. The Service Provider does not sell your data or share it with any other third party.

Beyond AI features, no data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Third-Party AI Services (OpenAI) – Data Disclosure & Your Consent

The Application uses artificial intelligence features powered by OpenAI, L.L.C. ("OpenAI"), 3180 18th St, San Francisco, CA 94110, USA. AI requests are routed through the Service Provider's backend server; you do not need to provide an OpenAI API key.

What data is sent: When you actively use AI-assisted features, the following data is transmitted to the Service Provider's backend and then forwarded to OpenAI's servers:

  • Camera images (resized) — when you tap "Photo" or "Text".
  • Voice transcriptions (text only, transcribed on-device) — when you use Follow-Up mode.
  • Generated text for speech synthesis — forwarded to OpenAI's TTS API for spoken output.

No other personal data (e.g., your name, email address, location, or device identifiers) is sent to OpenAI.

Who receives the data: Content is sent exclusively to OpenAI, L.L.C. via the Service Provider's backend. It is not sold or shared with any other third party.

How the data is collected: Data is transmitted only when you actively interact with AI-powered features. The Application does not passively collect or transmit data in the background.

Your permission – explicit consent required: Before you use the Application for the first time, it presents a clear, prominent notice explaining exactly what data will be sent and to whom, and asks for your explicit consent. No data is transmitted without your prior, active agreement.

Purpose: Your input is transmitted solely to fulfil your request for an AI-generated response. OpenAI processes the data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and Usage Policies.

Data retention: The Service Provider does not store images, voice transcriptions, or AI-generated text on its servers. Usage logs store only token counts and cost estimates — no content. Retention of content data is governed solely by OpenAI's own policies.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The backend uses industry-standard security practices including HMAC request signing, short-lived JWTs, and encrypted database connections. Only anonymised device data (as described above) is stored; no images or content are retained.
